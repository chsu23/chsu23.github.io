Bio

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at Rutgers University-Camden with Dr. Youwen Zhang. I received my Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering from the University at Buffalo under Dr. Yun Wu's supervision, where I developed biosensors for exploring extracellular vesicle biomarkers for cancer detection and treatment monitoring. My current research focuses on selective extracellular vesicle isolation, nanopore sensing, and targeted nanomaterials for cancer treatment/bone marrow conditioning.

Research Interests

  • Extracellular vesicles: Selective isolation and cancer biomarkers analysis
  • Biosensor development: Label-free electrical detection of extracellular using Nanopore and SPR biosnesor vesicles
  • Targeted nanomedicine: X-ray–activated nanomaterials for cancer treatment/bone marrow conditioning

Research Projects

  • Exo-PROS liquid biopsy biosensor

    Designed the surface plasmon resonance sensing architecture, engineered the capture surface, and built the data analysis workflow. Benchmarked the assay against ELISA, qRT-PCR, and Western blot.

  • Extracellular vesicle biomarkers for cancer diagnosis and treatment monitoring

    Evaluated extracellular vesicle biomarkers in cohorts of patients with breast or lung cancer and investigated whether early changes in vesicle biomarkers could help predict response to immune checkpoint inhibitors.

  • Programmable extracellular vesicle enrichment

    Developed a programmable nanopore-based workflow for selective enrichment of extracellular vesicle subpopulations for biomarker and functional studies. The platform can process up to 100 mL of sample.
