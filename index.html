Bio

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at Rutgers University-Camden with Dr. Youwen Zhang. I received my Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering from the University at Buffalo under Dr. Yun Wu's supervision, where I developed biosensors for exploring extracellular vesicle biomarkers for cancer detection and treatment monitoring. My current research focuses on selective extracellular vesicle isolation, nanopore sensing, and targeted nanomaterials for cancer treatment/bone marrow conditioning.
