Bio

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Rutgers University–Camden. I received my Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering from the University at Buffalo, where I developed biosensors for extracellular vesicle biomarkers in cancer. My current research focuses on selective extracellular vesicle isolation, solid-state nanopore sensing, and targeted nanomaterials for bone marrow conditioning.

Research Interests

  • Extracellular vesicles: Selective isolation and cancer biomarkers
  • Nanopore sensing: Label-free electrical detection of extracellular vesicles
  • Targeted nanomedicine: X-ray–activated nanomaterials for bone marrow conditioning
